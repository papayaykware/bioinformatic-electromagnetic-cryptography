Biological systems do not merely exchange molecular messages; they operate through coherent field architectures capable of fragmenting, protecting, and conditionally integrating information. This whitepaper develops the concept of **bioinformatic electromagnetic cryptography**, proposing exosomes as hybrid vectors whose functional content depends on receptor field coherence. Integrating advanced neurobiology, field physics, and the METFI–TAE frameworks, the organism and the Earth are treated as coupled toroidal systems operating far from equilibrium. Informational fragmentation is reframed as a cryptographic strategy, not a loss mechanism, yielding a naturally post-quantum security model grounded in physical irreproducibility rather than algorithmic complexity.
